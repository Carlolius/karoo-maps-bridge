# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-10)

First public release. Both apps tested on a Karoo 2 (Karoo OS 1.613) and an Android phone.

- Share places from Google Maps and other map apps; the Karoo lists the phone's points and opens
  the chosen one in its native "navigate to / save as POI" screen.
- Google Maps directions: send the stops in order, or compute BRouter bike route alternatives
  (normal, fast, quiet), drawn on a map with distance, climbing, time, surface and cycle lanes,
  and upload the chosen one through the Hammerhead Companion app.
- Formats: Google Maps (pins, places, directions, short links), `geo:`, Apple Maps, Waze,
  OpenStreetMap, OsmAnd, Organic Maps / MAPS.ME.
- The phone's server stops by itself; the Karoo finds the phone via the hotspot gateway, a
  manual IP or the last phones that answered.
- English and Spanish.

Pre-release versions (0.1 to 0.4) were tested privately.
